# Product

Game Room Manage is management software for gaming cafes. Customers buy time, PCs run a Windows kiosk that counts it down, and staff run the room from a web panel.

## Audiences

- **Club owners** evaluate the software on the landing page and contact us by phone or email.
- **Players** reserve a PC or console on the landing page, with no account.
- **Staff** work in the admin panel during opening hours (14:00 to 02:00).

## Product truth for marketing

- PCs have live time tracking through the kiosk. Consoles are in the booking calendar only.
- The admin panel and kiosk support Georgian, English and Russian. The landing page adds Turkish and German.
- There are no published prices, customer counts or testimonials. Do not invent them.
- Screenshots and videos must use synthetic data, never real customers.
